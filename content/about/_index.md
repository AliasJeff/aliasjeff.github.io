## Zhexun Chen (陈喆勋)

M.S. student in Software Engineering at Xidian University. My research focuses on recommender systems, particularly generative recommendation, retrieval-augmented learning, and temporal interest modeling. I previously interned as a full-stack developer at [Taelor](https://taelor.style/) and as a backend developer at Tencent IEG, and I am pursuing my graduate research under the supervision of Prof. Guangneng Hu.

---

### Research Interests

- **Generative Recommendation** — semantic identifier representation, permutation-invariant set encoding, contrastive learning for representation alignment
- **Retrieval-Augmented Recommendation** — transferring LLM semantic knowledge into generative recommender frameworks
- **Sequential Recommendation** — modeling interest structure, drift, and multi-scale temporal dynamics

Recent work has been submitted to KDD 2027 and AAAI 2027. See [Papers](/papers) for details.
