<h3 class="paper-title"><a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=RctFyHzYDO">SECL: Semantic Equivalency Learning for Permutation-Invariant Generative Recommendation</a></h3>
<p class="paper-authors">Zhexun Chen, Haolin Song, Anwei Luan, Yuxun Zhang, Han Wang, Guangneng Hu</p>
<p class="paper-abstract">Proposes a semantic supervision framework that treats different permutations of the same semantic identifier set as equivalent views and aligns them via a contrastive objective. Improves NDCG@5 by up to 28% and representation uniformity across four benchmarks, with consistent gains on long-tail items and sparse users.</p>
<p class="paper-keywords">generative recommender · permutation-invariant · semantic equivalency · contrastive learning</p>
</article>

<h3 class="paper-title"><a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=4vw9VH4WkX">RG²: Retrieval-to-Generation Semantic Transfer for Generative Recommendation</a></h3>
<p class="paper-authors">Zhexun Chen, Guangneng Hu</p>
<p class="paper-abstract">Transfers semantic knowledge from LLM retrievers into generative recommenders through a lightweight adaptor and contrastive alignment, bypassing the expensive generative encoder. Achieves up to 29.2% improvement over the backbone with 6.6% additional training overhead.</p>
<p class="paper-keywords">generative recommendation · retrieval-augmented · LLM · contrastive learning</p>
</article>

<h3 class="paper-title"><a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=doln2LFt0g">TEMPO: Structured Multi-Scale Interest Dynamics for Continuous-Time Sequential Recommendation</a></h3>
<p class="paper-authors">Yuxun Zhang, Zhexun Chen, Guangneng Hu</p>
<p class="paper-abstract">Models interest structure via time-aware hypergraphs, interest drift via transition flow fields, and multi-scale temporal dynamics through decomposed long/short-term fusion. Effective across five real-world benchmarks for continuous-time sequential recommendation.</p>
<p class="paper-keywords">sequential recommendation · hypergraph · interest dynamics · continuous-time</p>
